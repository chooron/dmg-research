# Figure 9 Caption Draft (Results 3.5 / R5: Cross-Model Replication)

**Figure 9. Cross-model structural replication of snow-modulated hydrograph timing signals across three distinct hydrological host models (XAJ, GR4J, and SIMHYD).**

**(a)** Primary timing effect $\Delta |CT|^{\mathrm{Base}-\mathrm{CN}} = |CT|_{\mathrm{Base}} - |CT|_{\mathrm{CN}}$ (days) along the snow-activity gradient (S1–S5) for XAJ (orange, `#D55E00`), GR4J (green, `#009E73`), and SIMHYD (blue, `#0072B2`) under independent calibration (IC, solid circles) and differentiable parameter learning (dPL, open triangles). Positive values denote that incorporating explicit snow accumulation–melt dynamics ($\mathrm{CN}$) reduces absolute center-of-timing ($CT$) error relative to the unrepresented baseline ($\mathrm{Base}$). Markers denote stratum medians, and error bars denote 95% deterministic percentile bootstrap confidence intervals (1,200 resamples). Sample sizes ($N$) for the five frozen snow-activity strata ($f_{\mathrm{snow}}$) are: S1 ($[0, 0.05)$, $N=165$), S2 ($[0.05, 0.15)$, $N=156$), S3 ($[0.15, 0.30)$, $N=121$), S4 ($[0.30, 0.50)$, $N=34$), and S5 ($[0.50, 1.00]$, $N=55$), annotated on the XAJ panel; all three host models share the identical 531-basin sample and primary estimand. Shared horizontal axes are maintained to facilitate magnitude comparability; magnitude differences across host structures reflect inherent structural sensitivities and do not constitute a model performance ranking. The shaded vertical band denotes the pre-specified $\pm 5$-day timing equivalence interval.

**(b)** Continuous Spearman rank correlation ($\rho$) between basin snow fraction ($f_{\mathrm{snow}}$) and primary timing improvement $\Delta |CT|^{\mathrm{Base}-\mathrm{CN}}$ across all 531 basins for each host–regime combination (colored by host: XAJ orange, GR4J green, SIMHYD blue). Markers and horizontal error bars denote point estimates and 95% bootstrap confidence intervals.

**(c)** Basin-level transition distributions of $\Delta |CT|^{\mathrm{Base}-\mathrm{CN}}$ for non-snow (S1, orange), transitional (S3, green), and high-snow (S5, blue) regimes. Split violins depict kernel density estimates for IC (left half) and dPL (right half); markers denote stratum medians, and bold percentages indicate the proportion of positive timing improvements ($P(\Delta |CT|^{\mathrm{Base}-\mathrm{CN}} > 0)$). The light shaded horizontal band denotes the pre-specified $\pm 5$-day timing equivalence zone. For visual clarity, the display is clipped to $(-50, 100)$ days (12 of 2,046 basin-level points, or 0.58%, fall outside the display range); all kernel densities, medians, and proportions are strictly computed over the complete, unclipped sample.

**(d)** Absolute signed timing error ($CT_{\mathrm{sim}} - CT_{\mathrm{obs}}$, days) in high-snow basins (S4+S5, $f_{\mathrm{snow}} \ge 0.30$, $N=89$) across structural arms: Base (orange; `#D55E00`), TGD (green; `#009E73`; temperature-conditioned generic control), and CN (blue; `#0072B2`; explicit snow accumulation–melt), aligned with the canonical paper palette in Figures 1–4. Points denote medians and whiskers denote interquartile ranges (IQR, 25th–75th percentiles). The horizontal sequence of Base, TGD, and CN illustrates descriptive progression; TGD is a generic temperature control rather than a mathematically nested intermediate model between Base and CN, and residual timing differences ($\mathrm{CN} - \mathrm{TGD}$) must not be interpreted as isolated process attribution. For display resolution, the vertical axis is clipped to $(-90, 35)$ days (22 of 1,602 basin evaluations, or 1.37%, fall outside the display range; summary statistics reflect all 89 basins without omission).

**(e)** Cross-host replication coherence composition across snow strata S1–S5. Stacked bars represent the mutually exclusive fraction of basins exhibiting positive timing improvement in all 3 hosts (deep blue; `#1F4E79`), exactly 2 of 3 hosts (medium blue; `#6BAED6`), or $\le 1$ host (light neutral grey; `#D0D5DA`). Top annotations report the combined multi-host replication probability $P(A \ge 2) = P(\text{exactly 2/3}) + P(\text{all 3})$, with error bars showing its 95% confidence interval attached to the cumulative $P(A \ge 2)$ bar boundary. $P(A \ge 2)$ represents supporting descriptive replication evidence across distinct model structures rather than a formal hypothesis test of three independent Bernoulli trials.
