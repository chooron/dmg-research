# Figure 8 Caption Draft (Results 3.4 / R4: Spring Soil-Water Recharge Timing)

**Figure 8. Soil-water recharge timing signatures under explicit snow vs. generic temperature compensation in snow-influenced catchments.**

**(a)** Daily external snow water equivalent (SWE, mm) for illustrative catchment 09306242 during water year 2004, showing the active-melt recharge period (Phase 2 shaded in light grey).

**(b)** Standardized calendar-month anomalies of simulated total soil water ($W_{\text{total}}$) for Base (orange), TGD (green), and CN (blue) alongside the ERA5-Land $\text{SM}_{100}$ reference (black).

**(c)** Spring zoom (Jan–Jun) illustrating the timing identification of spring wet-up and peak soil-water anomalies across models and reference.

**(d)** Basin-level spring wet-up timing error distributions ($\Delta \text{DOY}$, days) relative to $\text{SM}_{100}$ in high-snow (Q3) catchments.

**(e)** Empirical cumulative distribution functions (ECDF) of peak-timing offsets relative to ERA5-Land $\text{SM}_{100}$ in Q3 snow-active catchments ($N = 133$).

**(f)** Timing-definition sensitivity of mean absolute error (MAE) reduction relative to Base (days; $\text{MAE}_{\text{Base}} - \text{MAE}_{\text{Model}}$, positive values denote improved timing alignment) across varying spring wet-up windows (7 d, 14 d, 21 d) and annual vs. spring-summer peak definition windows for IC (fused) and dPL (seed 42).

*Caption Note on Conditioning*: External-SWE-active water years are defined by annual maximum SWE $\ge 5\text{ mm}$ from Snow-17 forcing without imposing internal model $W_{\text{total}}$ thresholds. High-snow catchments (Q3) represent basins in the upper quartile of external SWE burden.
