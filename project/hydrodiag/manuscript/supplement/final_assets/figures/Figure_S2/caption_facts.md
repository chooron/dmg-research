# Caption facts — Figure S2

## Panel a
- Residence time `tau_t` is plotted against temperature on the frozen 351-point response grid.
- Six response settings are shown: default, fitted P10, fitted median, fitted P90, upper bound, and lower bound.

## Panel b
- Daily retention `r_t` is plotted against temperature for the same six settings.
- The response is continuous in temperature.

## Panel c
- Median `Delta F = F_TGD* - F_close` is shown for Sharp, Canonical, Warm-shifted, and Broad settings.
- IC is open; dPL is filled.

## Sample
- Panels (a)–(b): 351 temperatures.
- Panel (c): denominator-valid basin metrics, canonical N = 427 IC and 460 dPL.

## Symbols
- `T_ref` and `s_T` are fixed gate constants in the canonical TGD formulation.
- `r_t = exp(-1/tau_t)`.

## Uncertainty
- Panel (c) intervals are empirical Q25–Q75 across the source basin-level metrics.

## Visual encodings
- Teal/green line family; line style identifies response setting.
- Horizontal zero reference is retained.

## Interpretation qualifier
- Response-shape sensitivity within the frozen TGD control family; not a physical snow model or universal bound.
