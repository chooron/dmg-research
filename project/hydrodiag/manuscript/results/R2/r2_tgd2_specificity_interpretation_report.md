## 3. Classification (pre-registered rules applied)

**Classification: `PARTIAL / QUALIFIED`**

- **Primary IC anchor — rule 2 condition holds (NOT supportive of specificity).** The IC
  Base–CN and Base–TGD2 snow-gradient slopes are close (+0.154 vs +0.148 Full; +0.139 vs
  +0.124 Excl-S5) and both paired slope-difference CIs cross zero ([−0.025, +0.038] Full;
  [−0.017, +0.048] Excl-S5). Under the pre-registered rules this means the R2 parameter-
  space evidence does **not** support the claim that the explicit snow-process structure
  produces a *more distinct* snow-organized parameter-space contrast in the primary,
  independent IC anchor.
- **Secondary dPL anchor — rule 1 condition met in Full only.** The paired difference is
  positive with CI excluding zero at Full (+0.079 [+0.045, +0.113]) but crosses zero at
  Exclude-S5 (+0.062 [−0.006, +0.132]). Per the pre-registered regime weighting this is a
  qualified expression under the shared parameter mapping (frac_snow is a dPL attribute
  input), not an independent environmental discovery, and it is not robust to S5 exclusion.
- Because the result falls between a clean supported difference (rule 1) and a clean null
  (rule 2) — the secondary anchor supports it, the primary anchor does not — the result is
  classified **PARTIAL / QUALIFIED** with no binary packaging.

### Consequence for R2 writing (per pre-registration)

- R2's main claim remains: **structure-conditioned, snow-organized parameter
  reorganization** — the Base–CN parameter-space trace strengthens with snow-process
  activity and is robust to Exclude-S5 in both regimes.
- The claim is **not** upgraded to snow-process specificity on the strength of the R2
  parameter-space evidence; process specificity is carried by R1 outlet-level evidence and
  future R3. This is stated explicitly and is not hidden or softened.
- A qualified tendency is noted: under the dPL shared mapping, Base–CN excess slopes exceed
  Base–TGD2 slopes with a paired CI excluding zero at Full (n=531), consistent with (but not
  proof of) a more snow-organized contrast for the explicit-snow structure; the tendency
  does not survive Exclude-S5 with CI excluding zero.
- TGD2–CN: computed fully; its snow gradients are positive and intermediate between
  Base–TGD2 and Base–CN in both regimes. It is highly redundant with the two primary
  contrasts (all show the same positive snow organization) and is therefore reported in
  Table/Supplement rather than the main figure (pre-registered §6).

## 4. Writing note: weak IC 15D global excess vs strong parameter-level gradients

IC's 15D global excess is small (Full median +0.0075) while several single-parameter snow
gradients are pronounced. These are not contradictory: the 15D RMS excess is a conservative
all-parameter summary, and dimensions without systematic directional signal dilute the few
stable directional signatures when aggregated in RMS. Parameter-specific evidence is
presented independently (Figure 4). This is a measurement/aggregation interpretation; no
claim is made that the remaining parameters are "pure noise", and no post-selected
low-dimensional distance is introduced.
