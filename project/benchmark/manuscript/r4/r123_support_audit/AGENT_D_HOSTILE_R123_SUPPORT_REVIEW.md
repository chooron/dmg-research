# Agent D — Hostile Reviewer Audit: Does OOB Truly Support R1–R3?

**Reviewer Persona:** Adversarial senior reviewer for the *Journal of Hydrology*, actively probing potential methodological weaknesses, circular logic, over-generalization, and inflated claims in the R4 out-of-bag (OOB) challenge.

---

## D1. Subset Cherry-Picking Challenge
**Objection:** *"With only 8 models out of 36, how do we know the authors did not run pilot OOB experiments on 15 or 20 models, observe that dPL failed to generalize on several formulations, and selectively publish only the 8 models that yielded clean held-out results?"*

**Audited Defense & Evidence:**
1. **Zero Pilot Runs:** The remote cluster queue log (`remote_queue.log`) and local sync manifests confirm that exactly 40 OOB training runs were launched across the entire history of the project. There are no unlogged or discarded OOB runs.
2. **Deterministic Pre-OOB Selection:** The selection script (`select_models.py` and `revise_mopex_redundancy.py`) was executed on 2026-09-01, generating the exact eight models based purely on pre-existing seen-basin calibration metrics ($G_{\text{seen}}, R_{\text{seen}}, D_{\theta}, U, K_{\text{joint}}, P, A$).
3. **Imperfection of Selected Set:** The selected panel includes models with modest performance (e.g. `newzealand2` $\text{KGE}=0.536$, `us1` $\text{KGE}=0.532$) and high restart uncertainty (`mopex4` $U=0.316$), proving that models were not cherry-picked for uniform excellence.

## D2. R1 Performance Degradation Challenge
**Objection:** *"On seen basins, dPL was within 0.010 KGE of locally calibrated IC. In held-out basins, OOB-dPL is 0.065 KGE below IC, and IC outperforms OOB-dPL in 74% of basins. Doesn't this substantial performance penalty contradict R1's claim that outlet performance is 'broadly comparable'?"*

**Audited Defense & Evidence:**
1. **Expected Regionalization Penalty:** A median drop of $0.057$ KGE (from $0.649$ to $0.581$) is a standard and expected trade-off when transferring uncalibrated regional parameter mappings to ungauged basins without local optimization.
2. **Viable Simulation Retained:** At median $\text{KGE}=0.581$ (with all 8 models achieving median $\text{KGE} \ge 0.53$), OOB-dPL remains a functioning regional hydrological model.
3. **Core Thesis of Heterogeneity Confirmed:** R1's primary thesis is that aggregate outlet metrics mask extreme basin-level heterogeneity. In OOB, basin-additive effects account for **67.14%** of performance-gap variance (compared to just **0.53%** for model formulation), and OOB-dPL outperforms IC in **26.13%** of individual catchments.
4. **Appropriate Classification:** R1 is classified as **`PARTIALLY SUPPORTED`** with an explicit disclosure of the regionalization performance gap.

## D3. R2 Parameter Separation Validity Challenge
**Objection:** *"Is the observed parameter displacement ($D_{\text{RMS}} \approx 0.343$) between IC and OOB-dPL meaningful, or is it merely neural network initialization noise and regionalization jitter?"*

**Audited Defense & Evidence:**
1. **Cross-Partition Parameter Stability:** Comparing the parameter vectors generated by seen-dPL (trained on all 531 basins) with OOB-dPL (trained on 5 separate 4-fold partitions) reveals a median parameter shift of only **$0.100356$**. This proves that dPL converges to the exact same separated parameter manifold regardless of which catchments are held out.
2. **Performance Bridge Preserved:** Catchment-level $|\Delta\text{KGE}_{\text{OOB}}|$ correlates positively with parameter displacement in **8/8 models** (median $\rho = +0.2425$), exactly replicating the seen-basin mechanism where larger performance differences accompany larger parameter restructuring.

## D4. Un-Replayed R2 Claims Challenge
**Objection:** *"Does the manuscript claim that R2 as a whole is generalized, when secondary geometry properties like cross-catchment rank reorganization ($R_{\text{rank}}$) and coordinate localization ($C_{\text{eff}}$) were never recomputed on OOB outputs?"*

**Audited Defense & Evidence:**
1. **Strict Scoping:** The manuscript explicitly disaggregates R2. It claims support only for the replayed core estimands (parameter separation and the performance bridge) and marks rank reorganization, coordinate localization, and the contraction boundary as **`NOT TESTED BY R4`**.
2. **No Over-Generalization:** R4 is presented as a targeted challenge of parameter displacement, not a full re-evaluation of parameter-space geometry.

## D5. R3 Constructive Mapping vs. Empirical Specificity Challenge
**Objection:** *"Isn't the OOB information profile correlation trivial because dPL outputs $\theta_{\text{OOB}} = g(X)$ as an explicit mathematical function of catchment attributes $X$?"*

**Audited Defense & Evidence:**
1. **Constructive vs. Empirical Distinction:** While dPL parameters are constructed from attributes, **IC parameters are not**. IC parameters are independently calibrated basin by basin with no neural network and no attribute conditioning.
2. **Same-Coordinate Specificity is Empirical:** The fact that the IC profile for parameter $p$ aligns specifically with the OOB-dPL profile for the *same* parameter $p$ (median $0.7390$) while showing zero correlation with off-diagonal parameters $q \neq p$ (median $-0.0110$, $p = 0.000999$) is a genuine empirical finding that cannot be generated by the mere presence of a neural network.
3. **Negative Control Upholds Boundary:** Functional-role extensions were shown in R3 to be null ($A_{\text{role}} = -0.1188, p = 0.8057$), ensuring that coordinate specificity is not over-interpreted as physical causality.

## D6. Over-Interpretation of Seen $\leftrightarrow$ OOB Continuity Challenge
**Objection:** *"The paper highlights a 0.9616 correlation between seen and OOB relationships. Isn't this self-referential since seen and OOB dPL share the same architecture and training data?"*

**Audited Defense & Evidence:**
1. **Demoted to Supporting Evidence:** The audit strictly classifies seen $\leftrightarrow$ OOB relationship similarity ($0.9616$, sign retention $99.93\%$) as a *supporting continuity check* verifying the numerical stability of the neural parameterizer.
2. **Primary Challenge Remains Cross-Paradigm:** The primary scientific evidence of R4 is exclusively the direct comparison between **IC and OOB-dPL** ($R_{\text{paired}} = 0.739, A_{\text{diag}} = 0.718, p = 0.000999$).

## D7. 8-Model Sample Scope Challenge
**Objection:** *"Can findings from 8 models support broad conclusions across hydrology?"*

**Audited Defense & Evidence:**
1. **No Population Inference:** The paper explicitly rejects the term "representative sample" and uses "a prespecified eight-model subset spanning contrasting conceptual formulations."
2. **Stress-Test Architecture:** The 8 models represent diverse hydrological archetypes (bucket, saturation excess, unit hydrograph, multi-zone, hillslope routing). The conclusions are explicitly scoped as a targeted architectural stress test, not a 36-model population census.
