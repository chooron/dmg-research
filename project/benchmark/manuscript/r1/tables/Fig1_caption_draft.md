# Figure 1 caption draft

**Figure 1. Similar aggregate streamflow performance masks substantial model–basin-specific differences between IC and dPL.** IC denotes the independently calibrated CMA-ES parameterization and dPL denotes the canonical differentiable parameter-learning parameterization. Throughout, `ΔKGE = KGE_dPL − KGE_IC`; negative values indicate higher IC KGE and positive values indicate higher dPL KGE. (a) Median test-period KGE across the 531 basins for each of the 36 model structures, shown as same-row paired IC and dPL points with connecting segments in canonical model order; open blue circles and filled coral squares distinguish the two estimators when values nearly overlap. Very light neutral-gray row shading identifies models where model-level median test KGE for dPL exceeds that for IC; this shading is descriptive and does not denote model suitability. Across-model medians are IC=0.622 and dPL=0.609, with median model-level ΔKGE=−0.010. (b) Within-model basin distributions of ΔKGE, with Q10–Q90 whiskers, Q25–Q75 intervals, medians, the vertical zero reference line (`ΔKGE = 0`), and the model-level `P_m+` column. Pale blue and coral background shading marks negative (`ΔKGE < 0`, IC higher) and positive (`ΔKGE > 0`, dPL higher) halves. Here `P_m+` is the fraction of the 531 basins for which dPL KGE exceeds IC KGE for a given model. (c) Basin-level summary of ΔKGE at 531 CAMELS-US gauges. Colours show the median difference across the 36 model structures (`M_b = median_m(ΔKGE_{m,b})`), and all map symbols have equal size. The spatially mixed pattern is descriptive and is not interpreted as spatial suitability or a preferred estimator. The inset compares the typical effect magnitude `|M_b|` with the median absolute deviation across model structures (`S_b = MAD_m(ΔKGE)`), used here as a descriptive measure of cross-model spread; the diagonal reference line denotes `S_b = |M_b|`. Colour limits are symmetric about zero and clipped at ±0.18 for display (13 basins below and 11 above the limits). These summaries describe the full evaluation period; basin-level split-period rank persistence was modest (Spearman ρ≈0.35). (d) Descriptive decomposition of ΔKGE variation into model additive effect (4.0%), basin additive effect (29.8%), and interaction plus residual variation (66.2%); slate horizontal bars show the shares of sum of squared deviations from the grand mean, and the 66.2% component denotes variation remaining beyond additive model and basin effects. (e) Model-level temporal persistence between the prespecified A (1995–2003) and B (2003–2010) partitions; neutral slate filled circles denote same-sign model summaries and open diamonds denote sign-changing summaries, with the extreme `topmodel` point labeled and identity and zero references shown. The A/B values were obtained from prior forward-only evaluation of frozen IC and dPL results, with no refit or retraining in this figure revision. No panel displays a performance ranking, significance test, or new robustness analysis.
